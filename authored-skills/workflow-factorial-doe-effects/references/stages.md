# Factorial effects stages and local record contract

Every checked-in response, expected artifact and benchmark question is a synthetic fixture. This reference specifies offline arithmetic acceptance only and does not establish observational validity.

## Invocation and immutable identities

Public entry points in codex_science.factorial_doe_pack: validate_factorial_doe_pack(bundle), compute_factorial_effects(table, rules, coding=None) and student_t_critical(df, level=0.95). The CLI accepts exactly one bundle argument:

```bash
python3 scripts/validate_factorial_doe_pack.py examples/workflow-factorial-doe-effects/pack.json
```

Acceptance prints JSON on stdout with exit 0. All refusals, including missing, extra and unknown arguments, print JSON containing status refused and finding on stderr with exit 2. Help is plain usage on stdout with exit 0. No output option exists. A second concurrent call independently reads immutable inputs. Interruption leaves every file, ref and record unchanged, but stdout may be partial; the coordinator must capture durable artifacts atomically. The validator writes nothing.

An artifact identity is exactly path and sha256, a canonical bundle-relative POSIX path and lowercase 64-hex SHA-256 of the bytes parsed. Absolute artifact paths, traversal, backslashes, symlinks, special files and noncanonical spellings refuse. Walk the absolute bundle root component by component using pinned directory descriptors, O_DIRECTORY, O_NOFOLLOW and O_NONBLOCK. Compare lstat/fstat identities and retain every ancestor device/inode. Read artifacts relative to those descriptors with lstat, nonblocking/no-follow open, descriptor fstat, a bounded read and a post-read signature check. Cache initial raw bytes and parsed JSON by device/inode, including hardlink aliases. Re-read each distinct inode once to recheck hashes, verify every alias signature and the entire ancestor chain at the end. Replacement, growth and ancestor/root swaps refuse; hashes describe the exact bytes parsed.

## Limits and types

Constants: MAX_BYTES 67108864 per file; MAX_TOTAL_BYTES 268435456 initial raw bytes across distinct inodes; MAX_NODES 4000000 JSON values across the bundle; MAX_ROWS 10000 generic JSON container entries; MAX_RECORDS 128 artifact paths, claims, evidence descriptors, total evidence records and citations; MAX_STRING 4096 characters; MAX_DEPTH 32; MAX_NUMBER 1e100 absolute generic numeric magnitude; MAX_FACTORS 4; MAX_RUNS 17; MAX_REPLICATES 128 per condition; MAX_OBSERVATIONS 4096 total responses. Each limit applies before work proportional to that dimension, including sets and cross products. Equality passes, excess refuses. A preparse upper allowance counts one root, commas and nonempty containers using an iterator; quoted punctuation may conservatively overcount. Hardlink aliases consume byte and parse allowances once. Malformed JSON, duplicate keys (nested too), NaN/Infinity, non-object roots, huge numbers, invalid UTF-8, lone surrogates and deep nesting yield labelled ValueError findings.

MAX_ITERATIONS 512 bounds incomplete-beta continued fractions; QUANTILE_STEPS 96 bounds t quantile inversion in the fixed 0 to 1000000 bracket. Nonconvergence refuses as t-convergence; no normal approximation fallback. Confidence levels are finite in 0.5 to 0.999 inclusive. Degrees of freedom are real integers in 1 to MAX_OBSERVATIONS. Seeds are real integers in 0 to 4294967295 inclusive. All declared integers, including generator signs, resolution, replicate counts, coded levels, report counts, interval df and alias signs, refuse bool, float and string. Strings are bounded, nonempty and validated before membership or dictionary use. The schemas have no schema_version field.

## Pack-local schemas

Each of the six records has schema equal to its name and fixture equal to synthetic fixture. Input objects have exact fields; missing or unknown fields refuse. These are ordinary domain artifacts, never new global receipts or sidecars.

- doe-pack-v1: rules, table and report artifact identities; evidence list of artifact identities; frozen_rules_sha256 equal to rules.sha256; real boolean frozen_before_responses_read true. Rules are loaded and validated and the freeze checked before table bytes are opened. Declarations cannot establish historical ordering.
- doe-rules-v1: factors, design, runs, response, units, effect_rule, error_rule, interval_level and randomisation_seed. Each factor is exactly id, low, high: IDs are ordered A through D and distinct low/high labels are bounded text. Design is exactly type, name, generators, resolution. Type is full or regular-fraction; names are 2^k or 2^(k-p), with actual integer counts substituted. Full designs have no generators and resolution zero. A regular fraction lists nonempty signed generators with factor, terms and sign; factor is distinct and terms are unique independent base factor IDs, with sign -1 or +1. Check the actual defining relation and its minimum nonempty word length against the integer stated resolution. Main columns must still be balanced and mutually orthogonal, so resolution-II fractions refuse.
- Frozen runs are exactly run_id, levels and replicates. Each condition appears once, with -1/+1 integer levels, except at most one centre condition with all zeros and its replicate count. Runs have unique IDs. Factorial replicate counts are equal, each integer from 1 to MAX_REPLICATES. The non-centre support is exactly the full base-factor product satisfying all generators. Replicate count one is admitted for descriptive contrasts, never for invented error inference. effect_rule is half-difference-of-level-means; error_rule is pure-error-from-replicates. response and units are nonempty text.
- doe-table-v1: rows and run_order. Rows are exactly run_id, levels and responses. Every frozen condition appears once, with its frozen levels and exact number of finite responses. Physical execution is not proven by the declared order. run_order is exactly the list of frozen run IDs shuffled with standard-library random.Random(randomisation_seed).shuffle; the randomized units here are condition groups, with replicates grouped within conditions. Do not claim this verifies laboratory randomisation or independent errors.
- doe-report-v1: rules_sha256, exploratory, coding, alias_sets, pure_error, curvature, summary, headline and claims. coding maps every factor to real integer +1 or -1. A sign reversal expresses post-outcome relabelling, requires exploratory true and recomputation, and retains the original frozen labels. Original frozen table coordinates always remain unchanged. All other choices remain frozen. Headline and claims are checked before response arithmetic so boundary refusals are not hidden by a later data error.
- doe-evidence-v1: records. Each evidence record is exactly id, kind, result, origin and source identity. IDs are unique across documents. Kind and result are bounded text. Origin is internal, external, independent or preregistered. Every non-internal source must differ by both path and hash from pack, rules, table, report and evidence documents. Hardlinks and content copies do not establish independence. Non-aliasing alone cannot prove independence.
- doe-result-v1: generated schema, fixture, status arithmetic-only, review unavailable, ordered stages, exploratory, immutable inputs, computation, claims and limitations. It is never a review receipt. expected.json wraps computation with the synthetic fixture label. negative-cases.json lists synthetic fixture mutations, bundle selection, target document/path, value/removal and distinct expected finding; tests materialize fresh hash-bound bundles for each.

Each alias-set output is exactly terms, estimate, standard_error and interval. Each term is exactly term and sign, relative to the first term's contrast; I denotes the intercept. All 2^k terms including I are partitioned into signed identical-column sets. No aliased member receives an additional independent estimate. Pure error is exactly df, sum_squares and variance. Summary is exactly factorial_runs, observations, centre_runs and grand_mean. A present curvature record is exactly estimate, standard_error, interval and departure. Intervals are exactly level, df, lower, upper and method student-t-pure-error. Without error degrees of freedom, variance, every standard error, interval and curvature departure are the string unavailable; no centre condition means curvature itself is unavailable. All reported numeric values must match with relative tolerance 1e-9 and zero absolute tolerance, including near-zero comparisons; integer types remain exact.

## 01-rule-freeze

Input: coordinator decision, question and factor/design choices. Call $cx-statistical-decision-analysis and $science-provenance to freeze rules.json with its SHA-256 as doe-rules-v1 and an existing manifest step. Output: immutable rules identity and one coordinator checkpoint. Fix levels, generators, resolution, counts, centre inclusion, response/units, half-difference rule, pure error, interval level and seed before outcomes. Stop on essential missing inputs or interpretation-changing choices. Preserve rejected alternatives and original revisions; no routine per-stage human gate.

## 02-table-qc

Input: rule identity and table.json path/hash. Call the validator identity/QC checks. Output: admitted doe-table-v1 plus preserved failure/control records as ordinary artifacts. Require unique run IDs, finite responses, frozen counts/levels, integer coding, exact zero column sums and zero pairwise dot products. Check complete design support and every signed generator exactly. Check run_order against the seeded permutation. Stop on any failed control; never repair or discard a row to obtain balance. Null/missing outcomes cannot stand in for observed responses and are refused rather than treated as negative evidence.

## 03-effects

Input: admitted rules/table identities and applied coding. Call compute_factorial_effects, student_t_critical and the one-argument CLI. Output: alias_sets, pure_error and curvature in doe-report-v1 and doe-result-v1. Compute each interaction sign as the product of its coded factors. With balanced factorial responses, each identifiable coefficient is sum(x*y)/N, exactly half the difference of level means. I is the grand mean; a defining word belongs to its intercept alias set. Negative generators retain signed aliases. Orthogonality is verified with exact integer arithmetic, and floating sums use math.fsum. Cache each condition response total once before iterating over contrast columns.

Pool within-condition squared deviations only: SSE = sum over every condition and replicate of (y - condition mean)^2; df = sum(n_condition - 1), including centre replicates. When df > 0, require strictly positive SSE and estimate MSE = SSE/df. For each factorial alias contrast SE = sqrt(MSE/N). Use an equal-tail Student t interval coefficient plus/minus t(df, (1+level)/2) times SE, found through incomplete-beta inversion. Do not substitute model residuals or aliased interactions for pure error. df zero yields unavailable errors/intervals and refuses significance, p-value or rejection wording. Degenerate variance refuses instead of returning a significant-looking quantity. Homoscedastic independent normal replicate errors are assumptions, not validated observations.

If a centre condition exists, always compute centre mean minus factorial grand mean, with SE = sqrt(MSE*(1/n_centre + 1/N)) and the same pure-error df and t interval. departure is detected only when the interval is strictly above or strictly below zero, otherwise not-detected. This is an unadjusted single curvature check, not a search over terms; without error it is unavailable. It cannot identify an individual quadratic coefficient or validate a mechanism. Keep the centre check even if the estimate is zero or the interval crosses zero.

## 04-summary

Input: all admitted conditions, complete alias sets, error and curvature artifacts. Call the reference summary comparison. Output: factorial/centre condition counts, total observations and factorial grand mean in doe-report-v1. Preserve all failed controls, negative results, conflicts, unavailable quantities, dependency assumptions, rejected alternatives and sensitivity needs. These are descriptive arithmetic checks, not optimal-condition selection, scale-up or process validity.

## 05-handoff and claim gate

Input: immutable report/evidence identities, all rules, table, failures and coordinator records. Call $science-provenance for ordinary artifacts and separate claim-register entries, then $science-review for record, source and method review. Output: doe-result-v1 and the existing coordinator checkpoint, claim register and later independent receipt. This pack cannot mark its own output reviewed or assign rigor. Failed controls and negative results stay in immutable input revisions and caller-captured refusal JSON.

Claims are exactly id, kind, status, text and evidence IDs. Kind is descriptive or associational. Status is planned, supported, replicated, suggestive, conflicting, unsupported, withdrawn or unavailable. An empty evidence list requires unavailable. Citing ANY unavailable kind or result requires unavailable for every other status, including mixed observed evidence. An unavailable claim cannot assert supported, replicated, suggestive or significant evidence. This synthetic fixture always refuses replicated. Every positive status refuses any citation whose kind OR result denotes review, approval, computation, numerical agreement, truth, physical or clinical validity. Labels never replace evidence.

The conservative case-insensitive English whole-word/prefix wording families are: caus*, mechanis*, therap*, clinic*, efficac*, effective*, treat*, cure*, prevent*, recommend*, prescri*, bind*, affinit*, inhibit*, activat*, regulat*, drive/drives/driv*, induc*. Additional refusals: truth, physical validity, patient benefit, optim*, scale-up and process validity. The gate includes negations and is incomplete for unlisted paraphrases or other languages. The same gate applies to headlines and claims regardless of kind. Association is never causality; computed agreement is never physical or clinical validity; absence of records is unavailable, never negative evidence; review approval is never truth.

Human gates apply only before installation, restricted transfer, new hosts, GPU/remote/heavy compute or an interpretation-changing choice. This pack performs none of the external operations. Independent review stays coordinator-owned and unavailable here; review approval cannot turn numerical agreement into truth.
