# Bland–Altman stages and local record contract

Every checked-in measurement, expected artifact and question is a synthetic fixture. This reference controls software arithmetic and traceability only. It supplies no evidence of observational validity or independent review.

## Invocation and immutable identities

Public entry points in codex_science.bland_altman_agreement_pack are validate_bland_altman_agreement_pack(bundle), compute_agreement(pairs, rules, maximum_allowed_difference=None) and student_t_pvalue(t, degrees_of_freedom).

```bash
python3 scripts/validate_bland_altman_agreement_pack.py examples/workflow-bland-altman-agreement/pack.json
```

The CLI takes one bundle argument, prints JSON on stdout and exits 0 on acceptance. All refusals, including missing, extra or unknown arguments, print JSON with status refused and finding on stderr and exit 2. Help is plain usage on stdout and exits 0. No output option exists. No file, record or ref is written. A second concurrent call independently reads the same immutable inputs. Interruption leaves every input unchanged and may truncate stdout; the coordinator must capture durable output atomically, then register it through existing provenance tools.

Every artifact identity is exactly path and sha256: a canonical bundle-relative POSIX path and lowercase 64-hex SHA-256 of the bytes parsed. The entry-point pack has no initial declared hash. Refuse absolute artifact paths, traversal, backslashes, control characters, noncanonical spellings, symlink components, devices, directories, FIFOs and unavailable paths. Walk from the filesystem root through directory descriptors with O_DIRECTORY, O_NOFOLLOW and O_NONBLOCK, recording every ancestor device/inode and comparing lstat with fstat. Retain the final root descriptor. Read artifacts relative to pinned descriptors with lstat regular-file validation, O_NONBLOCK and O_NOFOLLOW, descriptor fstat, a size-bounded read and post-read signature check. Cache bytes and parsed objects by device/inode so hardlink aliases share initial reads and parsing. Hash exactly those bytes. At completion re-read each distinct inode once, check every alias signature/hash and compare the complete ancestor chain twice. Refuse replacement, growth or root/ancestor substitution. Input defects are never silently repaired.

## Limits and types

Module constants: MAX_BYTES 67108864 per file; MAX_TOTAL_BYTES 268435456 aggregate initial bytes across distinct inodes; MAX_NODES 4000000 JSON values per bundle; MAX_ROWS 10000 generic JSON entries per container; MAX_UNITS 10000 frozen IDs, rows and reported units; MAX_REPLICATES 128 values per method per unit; MAX_RECORDS 128 claims, evidence artifact identities, total evidence records and citations per claim; MAX_STRING 4096 characters; MAX_DEPTH 32; MAX_NUMBER 1e100 absolute generic numeric magnitude. Enforce bounds immediately after list parsing and before sets, loops or recomputation that scale with them. Empty frozen IDs, rows and method arrays are refused. Claim/evidence lists may be empty. Equality at a limit is admitted; excess is refused.

Byte bounds are checked from fstat before reading. The bundle JSON allowance is enforced before json.loads with root plus commas plus the number of nonempty containers, counted by an iterator. Quoted punctuation may conservatively overcount. Enforce the remaining allowance before each new parse; hardlink aliases consume budgets once. Duplicate keys including nested keys, non-object roots, NaN, Infinity, overflowing floats, huge integers, malformed UTF-8 and deep nesting produce labelled ValueError findings. All identity strings are bounded, nonempty and free of surrogate characters. Integers are actual int, never bool, float or numeric string: minimum_pairs, interval_minimum_pairs, summary n/unavailable, per-unit replicate_count and both confidence/regression degrees_of_freedom. No schema_version field is added to domain records; the progressive-reference index and quality declarations use existing schema_version 1.

Measurement numbers are finite and have absolute magnitude at most MAX_NUMBER. Log-scale measurements must be strictly positive. minimum_pairs is 3..MAX_UNITS; interval_minimum_pairs is exactly 30; loa_multiplier is 1.96; interval_level is 0.5..0.999; proportional_bias_alpha is 0.001..0.5; both absolute moment bounds are 0..100; maximum_allowed_difference is 1e-100..MAX_NUMBER. Public t is finite within plus/minus MAX_NUMBER and degrees_of_freedom is an actual int in 1..MAX_UNITS. MAX_ITERATIONS 10000 bounds the incomplete-beta fraction; QUANTILE_STEPS 96 bounds t-quantile bisection. The confidence quantile uses a bounded 0..16 bracket suitable for the admitted n at least 30 and confidence range. No convergence failure falls back to a normal approximation.

REL_TOL 1e-9 with zero absolute tolerance compares reported numbers, frozen multiplier and changed bounds. Near-zero distinct numbers must differ. SINGULAR_REL 1e-12 defines relative degeneracy: sample SD of normalized differences and normalized means must exceed it; residual sum of squares must exceed SINGULAR_REL squared times the centered normalized difference sum of squares. Also refuse zero difference/mean scale, underflowed SD and underflowed nonzero t tails. Numerical sign and limit comparisons never use products of signs.

## Pack-local schemas

These six schemas are ordinary domain artifacts, not new global contracts. Every input has schema equal to its named schema and fixture equal to synthetic fixture. Unknown and missing fields are refused. Hash declarations preserve identity but cannot prove historical freeze timing or real-world independence.

- ba-pack-v1: rules, pairs and report artifact identities; evidence list of identities; frozen_rules_sha256 equal to rules.sha256; actual boolean frozen_before_values_read true. Rule validation and freeze check precede opening pair values.
- ba-rules-v1: unique nonempty unit_ids; reference_method A or B; common units text; scale original or log; difference_definition A - B; replicate_rule one-pair-per-unit or mean-per-method-before-transform; minimum_pairs; interval_minimum_pairs; loa_multiplier; interval_method bland-altman-1986-t; interval_level; proportional_bias_method exact-t-regression; proportional_bias_alpha; normality_max_abs_skewness; normality_max_abs_excess_kurtosis; maximum_allowed_difference. Numeric ranges are above. The reference method is recorded explicitly; difference sign remains A - B for either reference.
- ba-pairs-v1: nonempty rows. Every row is exactly unit_id, units, method_a and method_b. Every frozen ID appears once. Each method is a nonempty bounded list of numeric replicate values or null for a missing method. one-pair-per-unit requires each present list to have length one. The collapse rule averages each method's original-scale replicates before any log transformation; both present arrays must have equal counts. A missing method leaves the whole unit unavailable and excluded from arithmetic, while preserving its row. Do not impute zero, pair different units, silently discard an ID or count replicates as independent units.
- ba-report-v1: rules_sha256, actual boolean exploratory, maximum_allowed_difference, agreement_measure limits-of-agreement, scale, difference_definition, ordered units, summary, headline and claims. Scale and sign match frozen rules. An applied bound differing beyond REL_TOL requires exploratory true and recomputation, retaining original rules and hash. All other rules remain frozen. Header and claim gates run before numerical work so boundary/evidence findings precede later arithmetic errors.
- ba-evidence-v1: records list. Each record is exactly id, kind, result, origin and source. IDs are unique across documents. kind/result are bounded text. origin is internal, external, independent or preregistered. source is a path-plus-hash identity. A non-internal source path AND hash must differ from every supplied pack, rule, pair, report and evidence-document identity. Hardlinks and copies cannot establish independence. Non-aliasing does not prove actual independence.
- ba-result-v1: validator output schema, fixture, status arithmetic-only, review unavailable, ordered stages, exploratory, inputs path/hash identities, computation, claims and limitations. It is not a passed review receipt. computation is exactly units and summary. Each expected.json wraps computation and the synthetic fixture label. negative-cases.json contains fixture-labelled base prefixes and target/path mutations with replacement/removal and a distinct expected finding; tests materialize fresh hash-bound bundles.

Each computed/reported unit has exactly unit_id, status included or unavailable, integer replicate_count, difference and mean. Unavailable units have count zero and both numbers unavailable. The count denotes collapsed paired replicates, never independent n. Unit ordering follows the input table. Summary has exactly integer n and unavailable; bias; sd_difference; lower_limit; upper_limit; intervals; proportional_bias; normality; actual boolean limits_within_bound. Intervals is unavailable below 30 complete pairs; otherwise it has method, level, integer degrees_of_freedom, bias, lower_limit and upper_limit. Each interval endpoint object is exactly lower and upper. proportional_bias has method, slope, t, integer degrees_of_freedom, p_value and screen detected or not-detected. normality has skewness, excess_kurtosis and screen passed. Every reported scalar matches recomputation with REL_TOL only.

## 01-rule-freeze

Call $cx-statistical-decision-analysis and $science-provenance to preserve rules.json as ba-rules-v1 with its path/hash before values admission. Output: immutable rule identity, existing manifest plan step and one coordinator checkpoint. Freeze all handling rules, uncertainty, moment bounds, proportional-bias alpha and maximum difference. An interpretation-changing revision needs a human decision and exploratory labelling. No per-stage permission prompt.

## 02-pair-qc

Input: frozen rules and pairs.json path/hash identities. Call the pack validator's bounded file and pair checks. Output: admitted ba-pairs-v1 ordinary artifact with explicit missing methods and preserved exclusions. Require unique identities, matching units, finite numbers and strict positivity for logs. Repeated measurements require the declared collapse rule. Stop on identity/scale/control failure. Missing units must appear explicitly, not disappear from the denominator or become evidence of absence.

## 03-agreement

Input: admitted rule/table identities and declared applied bound. Call compute_agreement and student_t_pvalue through the one-argument CLI. Output: ba-report-v1 quantities and ba-result-v1 admission response. For each complete unit, collapse original replicates first. On original scale set d = A - B and m = (A+B)/2; on natural-log scale set d = log(A) - log(B) and m = (log(A)+log(B))/2. Keep all reported differences and bounds on the declared scale; do not compare log limits to original-unit bounds.

For n complete independent units, bias is sum(d)/n; sample SD s is sqrt(sum((d-bias)^2)/(n-1)); lower and upper limits are bias minus/plus 1.96s. Normalize before moments and regression to avoid scale-dependent underflow; use math.fsum. For n at least 30 use t at quantile (1+interval_level)/2 with n-1 degrees of freedom. Bias CI is bias plus/minus t*s/sqrt(n). Each limit CI is that limit plus/minus t*s*sqrt(3/n), the frozen Bland–Altman 1986 approximate variance convention. Below 30 all three CIs are unavailable, never a normal or invented interval. These are approximate intervals under normal independent differences, not prediction guarantees or validation of assumptions.

Regress d on m with an intercept: slope Sxy/Sxx; residual variance SSE/(n-2); slope SE sqrt(SSE/((n-2)*Sxx)); t is slope/SE with n-2 degrees of freedom. Report the two-sided Student t p value, computed via the regularized incomplete beta with bounded continued fraction; this is an exact t distribution calculation under the regression model, not an exact test without assumptions. screen is detected when p is strictly below frozen alpha, otherwise not-detected. Preserve detected bias and low power; never omit the check. Regression errors, independence, normality and constant variance remain assumptions. Singular means, differences or residuals refuse rather than producing a misleading number.

The frozen normality screen uses unadjusted standardized central moments: skewness = mean(((d-bias)/s)^3); excess_kurtosis = mean(((d-bias)/s)^4)-3 with sample SD s. Absolute moments must not exceed their predeclared bounds; equality passes. Failure is normality-screen and preserves the input/control result. The screen does not prove normality or constant variance. The descriptive bound fact is lower_limit >= -maximum_allowed_difference and upper_limit <= maximum_allowed_difference, including equality; it is not an acceptability claim.

Primary formula basis: Bland and Altman, Lancet 1986, 307–310, doi:10.1016/S0140-6736(86)90837-8; the author's explanatory note at https://www-users.york.ac.uk/~mb55/meas/sizemeth.htm. Student t distribution definition: https://itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/eda/section3/eda3664.htm. These identify the frozen convention, not evidence for the synthetic methods.

## 04-summary

Input: complete result, every unit row and immutable identities. Call reference report checks. Output: ba-report-v1 summary with complete-unit n, unavailable count, intervals and all screens. No test conclusion substitutes for limits or missingness. Preserve failed controls, null/negative results, exclusions, dependencies, assumptions, sensitivity needs, rejected alternatives and limitations. The next falsifiable action is an independently specified paired table with the same frozen rules; a new table is never automatically independent evidence.

## 05-handoff and claim gate

Input: report/evidence path-plus-hash identities, original rules and pairs, failures, reference-use-ledger and coordinator records. Call $science-provenance to register ordinary artifacts and separate claim-register entries; call $science-review in record, source and method modes. Output: ba-result-v1 and coordinator-owned existing checkpoint, claim register and later independent receipt. The pack never classifies rigor or marks its own output reviewed. Preserve failed inputs and caller-captured refusal JSON.

Claims are exactly id, kind, status, text and evidence IDs. Kind is descriptive or associational. Status is planned, supported, replicated, suggestive, conflicting, unsupported, withdrawn or unavailable. Empty evidence requires unavailable. Any unavailable evidence kind OR result requires unavailable, even with observed evidence and for every other status. An unavailable claim cannot assert supported, replicated, suggestive or significant evidence. Replicated is always refused: one synthetic fixture cannot establish two independent studies. Every positive status is refused when any citation's kind OR result denotes review, approval, computation, numerical agreement, truth, physical or clinical validity. Validate all status/kind/ID text before set/dictionary membership. Labels never substitute for evidence.

The conservative case-insensitive English whole-word/prefix wording families are: caus*, mechanis*, therap*, clinic*, efficac*, effective*, treat*, cure*, prevent*, recommend*, prescri*, bind*, affinit*, inhibit*, activat*, regulat*, drive/drives/driv*, induc*. Also refuse truth, physical validity, patient benefit, interchange*, equivalen* and method valid*. Any correlat* wording in a headline or claim is refused as correlation-as-agreement. The gate includes negations and is incomplete for unlisted paraphrases and other languages. Claim kind cannot bypass wording. Association is never causality; computed agreement is never physical or clinical validity; absence is unavailable, never negative; review approval is never truth.

Human gates apply only before installation, restricted transfers, new hosts, GPU/remote/heavy compute or interpretation-changing choices. This pack uses none of the installation/network/remote routes. Bounded arithmetic and unknown assumptions remain unreviewed; approval cannot turn them into truth.
